Welcome to Lantern Works

Everything you need for your first month, on one page.

01 First day

Come in at 9:30. Reception has your badge and a locker key. Your buddy for the month is Farah from the design desk; she will walk you to the floor.

The morning is set-up: laptop, accounts and a short safety walk. Lunch is on us in the canteen on level 2.

Arrive
9:30, main entrance
Bring
ID card, bank details

02 Tools

Mail, calendar and chat are set up before you arrive. Ask the help desk for the design suite or the data tools; requests are answered the same day.

Keep work files in the shared drive, not on the laptop. It is backed up every night.

Help desk
Ext. 2040
Hours
8:00 to 18:00

03 Leave

You start with 16 days a year, rising by one day each year up to 22. Book it in the staff portal at least two weeks ahead when you can.

Sick leave needs a note from a clinic after the first day. Tell your lead before 9:00.

Annual
16 days
Sick
14 days

04 Help

Your lead and your buddy are the first stop. For pay and contracts, write to the people team; they answer within two working days.

For anything you would rather not raise with your lead, the people team keeps it private.

People team
Level 3, room 12
Buddy
