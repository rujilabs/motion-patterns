Panalor 500 mg paracetamol tablets

Patient leaflet. Pack of 20, RM 6.80. Batch PN2611-047, use by 09/2028.

How to take it

Adults and children aged 12 and over: 1 to 2 tablets every 4 to 6 hours, with water. Do not take more than 8 tablets in 24 hours. Children aged 10 to 11: 1 tablet every 4 to 6 hours, no more than 4 doses in 24 hours. Not for children under 10: ask us about a syrup.

Do not take it

with any other medicine that contains paracetamol, including cold and flu remedies. Ask your pharmacist first if you have liver or kidney problems, drink more than 14 units of alcohol a week, or weigh less than 50 kg.

Side effects

are rare. Stop and see a doctor if you get a rash, peeling skin, mouth ulcers or unusual bruising. Taking too much can harm the liver even if you feel well: get help at once: call 999, or call us on 03-8945 2716.

In each tablet

500 mg paracetamol, with maize starch, povidone, stearic acid and talc. No sugar, gluten or lactose. Halal certified.

Pregnant or breastfeeding

It can be taken at the usual dose, for the shortest time you need it. Tell us before you buy.

Storage

Below 30 °C, in the original pack, out of the sight and reach of children. Do not use after the date on the strip.
