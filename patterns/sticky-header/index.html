A slow weekend on the Kuala Selangor river

Travel guide8 min read

Friday, late afternoon

Leave the city before four and you reach the river mouth as the light goes gold. Check in, drop the bags, walk to the jetty.

Friday night: the fireflies

The boats leave from the jetty after dark. No torches, no flash. The trees along the bank light up a few at a time, then all at once.

Saturday morning

Breakfast at the market by the bridge: roti canai, kopi o, and a bag of fried bananas for the road up the hill.

Saturday: the old fort

A short climb to the lighthouse and the old cannons. The silvered leaf monkeys know the visitors carry food. Keep your bag zipped.

Sunday, before you go

Seafood lunch at the riverside stalls, then home before the evening traffic.

End of the guide.
