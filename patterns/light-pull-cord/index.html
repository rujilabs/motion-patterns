Rendang tok, the Perak way

Serves 8 · Prep 40 min · Cook 3 h 20 min

You need

  • 1.2 kg beef shin, in 4 cm cubes
  • 400 ml thick coconut milk
  • 120 g kerisik, pounded fine
  • 15 dried chillies, soaked
  • 2 stalks lemongrass, bruised

Step 4 of 6

Keep the flame at its lowest and stir every 10 minutes. It is ready when the oil splits and the paste turns the colour of strong coffee, about 2 h 50 min in.
