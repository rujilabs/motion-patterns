Kancil Counts the Fireflies

At 7:45 pm the river went quiet, and Kancil crept out to the mangrove to count the fireflies before the crocodiles woke up.

One, two, three on the nipah leaf. Four by the old jetty. Five, six, seven in a line behind his tail, as if they wanted to come home with him.

Move your pointer, or drag a finger across the page. The stars and leaves follow.
