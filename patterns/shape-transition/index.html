Coconut rice, three steps

Serves 4, about 40 min

Step 1 of 3

Rinse the rice

Two cups, washed three times until the water runs almost clear.

Step 2 of 3

Simmer in coconut milk

With a knot of pandan leaf and a pinch of salt, lid on, low heat.

Step 3 of 3

Rest, then fluff

Ten minutes off the heat, then loosen with a fork and serve.
