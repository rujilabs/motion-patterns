Kopitiam Lam Heng, Jalan Pasar, Ipoh

A letter from the back kitchen

To everyone who sits at our marble tables,

My grandfather opened these shutters at 5:40 am on a Tuesday in 1961. A cup of kopi-o cost 15 sen, and he wrote every sale in a school exercise book that still sits behind the till.

We still roast our own beans with butter and sugar, 38 kg every Tuesday, in the same drum. The kaya is still stirred for four hours by hand. Today the kopi-o is RM 1.90, and I think he would forgive us.

Thank you for 63 years of mornings. Come early. The egg tarts are gone by 9:15.

Lam Mei FongThird generation, still behind the counter
