How we roast a bag of Kampung Gajah beans

Ipoh roastery6 min read

01

Green beans

Sorted by hand on the long table, then dried for three weeks under the shed roof.

02

First crack at 9 minutes

The beans pop like popcorn. We stop the timer by ear, not by the clock.

03

Two days of rest

Fresh beans are full of gas. They rest 48 hours before they go in a bag.

That is the whole roast.
