The riverside walk, in five stops

Four kilometres, flat the whole way. Two hours with stops.

Stops

  1. 01

    The old jetty

    Start where the ferry used to land. Kopi from the kiosk.

  2. 02

    Mangrove boardwalk

    Mudskippers at low tide. Stay on the boards.

  3. 03

    Iron bridge

    Built in 1931. Cross it for the view upstream.

  4. 04

    Fishing village

    Houses on stilts. Dried fish for sale by the steps.

  5. 05

    Lighthouse point

    End at sunset. The last bus back leaves at 7.40 pm.
