Before you sail

Yes, once per ticket, free until 2 hours before the sailing. Open My booking, enter your reference (it starts with LK) and pick a new time. Inside 2 hours it costs RM 5.00 at the Kuala Perlis counter.

One bag up to 20 kg goes free. Each extra kilo is RM 0.50 at the luggage counter. Bicycles travel on the 7:00 am and 3:30 pm sailings only, RM 12.00 each way.

We send an SMS at least 45 minutes before departure. Take the next sailing with seats free, or ask for a full refund to your card, which lands within 7 working days.

Children aged 3 to 12 pay RM 13.70 instead of RM 19.50. Seniors from 60 with a MyKad pay RM 15.60. Babies under 3 sail free on a parent's lap.

15 minutes before departure, and it does not reopen. Check-in opens 90 minutes before. Bring the MyKad or passport you booked with.

Still stuck? Counter staff at Kuala Perlis jetty, 6:30 am to 7:00 pm daily, or call 04-985 2216.
