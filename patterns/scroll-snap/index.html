1 of 4

Brew the tea strong

Two spoons of black tea dust to one cup of water. Boil for three minutes, then strain it through a cloth bag.

2 of 4

Add the milk

Sweetened condensed milk, two spoons per cup. Stir until the colour turns to a deep caramel.

3 of 4

Pull it, high

Pour from one mug to the other at arm's length, five or six times. The pull cools it and builds the froth.

4 of 4

Serve with the froth on top

Into a glass while it is still foaming. Kurang manis if you asked for less sugar.
