Collect a parcel at any Peti locker

Open 24 hours at 140 train stations and petrol stations.

How it works

  1. 1 of 3

    Open the code

    When the parcel lands, you get a message with a six-digit code and a QR.

  2. 2 of 3

    Scan or type it in

    Hold the QR under the scanner, or key in the six digits on the screen.

  3. 3 of 3

    Take it, close the door

    The door that opens is yours. Close it after, and the locker tells the sender.

Released. The page scrolls on as usual.

What if I miss the pickup?

Parcels wait three days, then go back to the sender's nearest hub.
